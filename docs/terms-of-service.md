## Trail information is a guide, not a guarantee

Tracknotes shows trail routes, distances, elevation, water sources, huts,
campsites, towns, map data from OpenStreetMap and others, plans and estimates
(walking times, water carries, resupply), and comments and waypoints posted by
other hikers. All of it can be incomplete, out of date or wrong. Tracks drift,
water sources dry up, huts close, routes are rerouted or closed, and
conditions change from day to day. Estimates are arithmetic on the figures you
enter, not advice about what you can safely do.

You are responsible for your own safety. Check official sources (land
managers, park and trail authorities, weather and fire services) before and
during a walk, carry a paper map, navigation and contingency water, and do not
rely on Tracknotes, on a phone or on a battery as your only means of finding
your way or water.

access other people's accounts or data.

## Moderation and takedown

Anyone can report a comment, shared waypoint or community route with the
**Report** action. We may review, hide, remove or decline to publish any
content, and may block a device or account from posting, at our discretion and
without notice — including content hidden automatically after several reports
or by the automated review of comments, shared waypoints and community routes.

To ask for something to be taken down (including a copyright complaint, or a
land manager asking us to remove a sensitive site), use **Report** or email
the address below. We aim to acknowledge requests within 72 hours. Requests
from land managers are honoured by default.

## Your account

Tracknotes has no sign-up: posting registers an anonymous device identity with
the display name you choose. You can delete it at any time from Settings →
**Delete account** (shown once you have posted); what that removes is described in the
[privacy policy](https://tracknotes.info/privacy.html). Content already
released under CC0 may remain in copies others have made.
