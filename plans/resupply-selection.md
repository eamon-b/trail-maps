# Resupply selection

Status: planned 2026-09-13; phases 1a, 1b, 1c, 2 and 3 implemented 2026-09-13 on `feature/load-cdt-data` (generator branches unpushed; mobile Phase 4 still open). Decisions taken with Eamon on the same day are marked **[decided]**;
questions still open are collected at the end.

## Why

On the long trails (CDT, Te Araroa) a resupply is a choice, not a fact. From Monarch Pass you can
hitch to Salida, to Poncha Springs, or walk on to the next town; from the Rangitata road end you can
go to Geraldine (70 km), Peel Forest (50 km), Mesopotamia (2.5 km) or Mt Potts (3 km). Choosing
which of those to take is the core of the planning stage, and the planner cannot express it: the
resupply list on both platforms is read-only and counts **every** resupply-family waypoint, so a
CDT plan shows 70 stops and a carry between every pair of them.

The feature: a checkbox list of resupply options on the plan page. Ticked options become the
stops of a resupply plan; the resupply datasheet recomputes the legs between them (distance,
ascent, descent, estimated days, food weight). It is a simpler sibling of the camp-stops list.

## What exists today (findings)

- **Web plan page** (`src/web/trails/plan-viewer.ts`, `plan-template.html`, duplicated as
  `src/web/my-plan.html`): left panel has two tabs, Days and Stops. Stops rows already look like
  checkboxes (`.stop-check` span, `renderStopList` :642-681) and toggle via `toggleStop` :778, which
  stores NOBO km in `PlanState.stops` and calls `scheduleSave()` + `renderAll()` :880. Resupply is a
  collapsible under the Days tab, `renderResupplySection` :590-612, fed by
  `analyzeResupply(waypoints, totalDistance)` with the default 20 km/day. Persistence is
  `localStorage` `trail-plan-<trailId>` via `plan-state.ts` with an `isValidPlanState` guard.
- **Calculator** (`src/lib/resupply-calculator.ts`): `extractResupplyPoints` filters by
  `isResupplyWaypoint(type)`; `computeResupplyGaps` is pure km subtraction, dedupes only
  adjacent equal km, days = `ceil(km / dailyKm)`, `isLong` = days > 5. No ascent, no ids, no
  route-break awareness (km itself is safe: a route break adds no distance to the cumulative
  scale, but any elevation must go through `calculateElevationBetween(..., breakStarts)`).
  `ResupplyGap` (`plan-types.ts:54`) has no ascent/descent fields.
- **Mobile** (`mobile/app/guide/[trailId]/plan.tsx`, `features/plan/`): no selection UI of any
  kind; `plan-adapters.ts:339` passes `trail.waypoints` straight into
  `analyzeResupplyForSection`, so a filter is a one-line change there. Only pace + daily hours
  persist (`plan-inputs-store.ts`, zustand + AsyncStorage, `version: 1`, has `migrate` and
  `clearTrail`). The closest checkbox idiom is `PoiLayersSheet.tsx` (`Switch` rows); there is no
  `accessibilityRole="checkbox"` anywhere yet.
- **Data.** The CDT GPX (`data/trails/cdt/cdt.gpx`, generated by `/mnt/data/projects/GPX/CDT`)
  writes each off-trail town twice: the town at its real position (lands in
  `offTrailWaypoints`, invisible to the planner) and an on-trail twin named
  `"<Town> (access: <trail feature>)"` **typed as the served type** (`town`, not `town-access`).
  Off-trail distance, hitch/shuttle/foot mode and "accepts boxes" are flattened into `<desc>`
  text ("Leave the CDT here for Augusta: 30 mi by hitching."). 13 turn-offs serve more than one
  town. Te Araroa (`/mnt/data/projects/GPX/nz`, published as `te-araroa-data`) has the same
  structured source (`accessFromKm`, `accessRoadKm`, `acceptsBoxes`) but emits **no** on-trail
  twin at all, so its 26 off-trail resupply points (Geraldine, Te Anau, ...) never reach the
  planner. No field for any of this survives `parseGpx` → `EnrichedWaypoint`
  (`trail-types.ts:95-117`); the parser reads only `name`/`desc`/`type`/`ele`.
- Resupply-family counts on trail today: AAWT 0, Hume & Hovell 2, Cape to Cape 3, Larapinta 6,
  Bibbulmun 13, Heysen 13, Te Araroa 59, CDT 70.

## Decisions

- **[decided] Web first.** Shared calculator in `src/lib` + the web plan page in this PR; mobile
  is a second PR that reuses the calculator and the same selection format (sketched in Phase 4).
- **[decided] Cluster grouping, tick any.** Options at the same turn-off are grouped under it,
  each with its own tick box. Ticking two in one group is allowed and counts as **one stop**.
- **[decided] Structured off-trail data goes into the build.** New optional waypoint fields
  (`offTrailKm`, `accessMode`, `acceptsBoxes`) carried in GPX `<extensions>` by the two
  generators and read by `parseGpx`. No regex over `<desc>` text.
- **[decided] Independent of the camp plan, but real leg days.** Ticking a resupply does not add
  a camp stop. Leg days come from Naismith hours over the track (route-break aware), not a flat
  20 km/day; legs carry ascent and descent.
- Assumed unless Eamon objects (see open questions): the default is **every option ticked**
  (today's behaviour); a persisted selection is an explicit list of waypoint ids; unknown ids are
  dropped silently on load.

## Data contract (Phase 1)

### GPX waypoint extensions

Both generators already hold the fields; they gain a small namespace on the `<wpt>`:

```xml
<gpx xmlns="http://www.topografix.com/GPX/1/1"
     xmlns:tn="https://tracknotes.app/xmlschemas/gpx-waypoint/1">
  <wpt lat="38.4967" lon="-106.3244">
    <name>Salida</name>
    <desc>Leave the CDT here for Salida: 22 mi by hitching. Walmart, Safeway, ...</desc>
    <type>town-access</type>
    <extensions>
      <tn:offTrailKm>35.4</tn:offTrailKm>
      <tn:accessMode>hitch</tn:accessMode>       <!-- foot | hitch | shuttle | boat | on-trail -->
      <tn:acceptsBoxes>true</tn:acceptsBoxes>
      <tn:accessName>Monarch Pass (US 50)</tn:accessName>
    </extensions>
  </wpt>
```

- The fast-xml-parser adapter already runs `removeNSPrefix: true` and DOM type selectors match on
  local name, so `wpt.querySelector('extensions')` → `querySelector('offTrailKm')` works on both
  backends without touching `XmlNode` (verified on both during review). Two parser rules follow:
  `textContent` is untrimmed, so every read site trims (`"\n true\n"` must equal `true`), and
  `offTrailKm` is guarded for NaN the way `parseElevation` is (`gpx-parser.ts:105-111`).
  `querySelector` is descendant-recursive, so the fields must be **direct children** of
  `<extensions>` and no other vendor block inside it may reuse these local names; the contract
  says so. The parity test (`xml-adapter.test.ts`) gets a fixture with extensions; its
  `flattenGpx` case (~:63) hardcodes four fixture names, so the new one is added there too (or
  the case switches to `fixtureNames`).
- `accessName` is the human name of the turn-off, used as the group header. Without it the
  group header falls back to the km.
- Everything is optional; a GPX without extensions parses exactly as today.

### Types and pipeline (this repo)

- `GpxWaypoint` (`src/lib/types.ts`) and `TrailWaypoint`/`EnrichedWaypoint`
  (`src/lib/trail-types.ts`) gain `offTrailKm?: number`, `accessMode?: AccessMode`,
  `acceptsBoxes?: boolean`, `accessName?: string`. Places that rebuild a waypoint with an explicit
  field list and must carry them: `flattenGpx` (`trail-ingest.ts:156-163`) and
  `enrichVariantWaypoints` (`trail-ingest.ts:786-802` — CDT alternates carry towns, so
  `VariantWaypoint` gets the same optional fields). Everything else spreads the object through
  (`enrichWaypoints`, the off-trail split, `applyElevation`, `waypoint-dedupe`, `trail-reverse`,
  `build-trails.ts:355-363`, `truncateWaypoint` in `build-mobile-trails.ts`). `PlanWaypoint`
  (`plan-types.ts:83`) gains `id?: string` and the four fields so the calculator sees them
  structurally. `parseHandoffJson` (`trail-handoff.ts:474`) spreads unknown keys through
  unvalidated; add a `finiteOr` line for `offTrailKm` beside the others.
- Invariant per napkin: build before/after and `diff -r public/data/generated` +
  `mobile/assets/trails` — the only differences may be the new fields on waypoints whose GPX
  carries extensions, and `git diff data/waypoint-ids.json` must be empty for every trail except
  the deliberate CDT migration below.
- `docs/gpx-import.md` §"What the types are used for" gets a short section on the extension
  fields (they are honoured on import too, so a hand-made GPX can declare a turn-off).
- Mobile's `slimPoi` is POI-only; nothing to do there. `build-mobile-trails.ts` copies waypoints
  whole, so the fields reach the phone for Phase 4 for free.

  Te Araroa's twins are new waypoints and simply append.

  would be offered as a place to sleep. No taxonomy or icon work is needed: the seven `-access`
  labels exist (`waypoint-taxonomy.ts:72-78`), `RESUPPLY_TYPES` already includes the three
  resupply turn-offs so counts do not change, and mobile's `waypoint-icons.ts` falls back through
  `baseWaypointType` to `town.png`.

## Shared calculator (Phase 2) — `src/lib/resupply-plan.ts`

Platform-neutral, DOM-free, no imports outside `src/lib`. Structural parameter types so web,
mobile and imports pass their own trail shapes.

```ts
export type AccessMode = 'foot' | 'hitch' | 'shuttle' | 'boat' | 'on-trail';

export interface ResupplyOption {
  id: string;             // waypoint id (registry id or uw_…); options without an id are skipped
  name: string;
  type: string;           // town | town-access | …
  km: number;             // active-direction km
  offTrailKm?: number;
  accessMode?: AccessMode;
  acceptsBoxes?: boolean;
  description?: string;
}

export interface ResupplyOptionGroup {
  key: string;            // stable: the first option's id
  km: number;
  label: string | null;   // accessName of the turn-off, or null when the group is a single on-trail town
  options: ResupplyOption[];
}

export interface ResupplyStop { km: number; name: string; optionIds: string[] }   // "Salida / Poncha Springs"

export interface ResupplyLeg extends ResupplyGap {
  ascentM: number;
  descentM: number;
  estimatedHours: number; // Naismith over the track, route-break aware
  estimatedDays: number;  // max(1, ceil(hours / dailyHours))
  food: FoodCarryEstimate;
  arrival?: { day: number; date?: string };  // from the camp plan when one exists
}

export function listResupplyOptions(waypoints, opts?: { groupWithinKm?: number }): ResupplyOptionGroup[];
export function resolveResupplyStops(groups, selectedIds: readonly string[] | undefined): ResupplyStop[];
export function computeResupplyLegs(trail: PlanTrail, stops: ResupplyStop[], opts: {
  dailyHours: number; baseKmh?: number; section?: SectionConfig | null;
  longThresholdDays?: number; gramsPerDay?: number; days?: ComputedDay[];
}): ResupplyLeg[];
export function summariseResupplyLegs(legs): { stops: number; longestKm; longestDays; totalFoodKg; hasData };
```

Rules:

- Options = on-trail `waypoints` with `isResupplyWaypoint(type)` and an `id`. Off-trail records
  and POIs are never options.
- Grouping: consecutive options whose km differ by ≤ `groupWithinKm` (default 0.1 km). A group's
  `label` is the first `accessName` found; a group of one plain town has `label: null`.
- `selectedIds === undefined` → every option ticked (default). Otherwise ticked = the ids present;
  unknown ids are ignored. Several ticked options in one group collapse into one `ResupplyStop`
  whose name joins theirs with " / ".
- Legs: Trail start → stop 1 → … → Trail end, exactly the boundary behaviour of
  `computeResupplyGaps`, scoped to `section` when given. Distance is km subtraction (the
  cumulative scale already excludes route-break gaps). Ascent/descent via
  `calculateElevationBetween(from, to, track.points, routeBreakStarts(track.breaks, 'points'))`,
  which also honours `cumAscent`/`cumDescent` on thinned mobile tracks. Hours via
  `estimateHikingHoursRaw` over the leg's ascent/descent. Food via `calculateFoodWeight`. Web
  tracks have no `cumAscent`, so each leg walks its points: the caller computes legs only when
  the selection, direction or section changes and caches the result (mirroring
  `cachedActiveStops`), never inside every `renderAll()`.
- `isLong` keeps the existing meaning (days > threshold, default 5).
- `arrival` is `correlateResupplyWithDays` when the caller passes `days`.
- Direction: callers pass the direction-applied trail, as `computeDays` does; ids do not change
  under reversal (`trail-reverse` spreads the objects), so a saved selection survives a
  direction flip without conversion. Legs come out in active-direction order.
- `resupply-calculator.ts` stays as is for mobile until Phase 4; nothing there changes shape.

Tests (`src/lib/resupply-plan.test.ts`, hand-written expected numbers): grouping at exactly the
threshold; two ticked in one group → one stop with a joined name; default-all vs explicit vs
unknown ids; a synthetic track with a route break asserting the ascent skips the gap and the km
does not include it; section scoping; days from hours at 8 h/day; `arrival` when `days` is given;
a real-shape regression on the built CDT (Monarch Pass group has three options, one stop).

  `#resupply-list` since the body is re-rendered.

### Right panel: the resupply datasheet

Today `initTabs` (`plan-viewer.ts:862-873`) only re-renders the stop list on a tab switch and
`renderAll` always calls `renderDayDatasheet`. The change: `initTabs` calls `renderAll()`, and
`renderAll` dispatches the datasheet on `activeTab` (`resupply` → the table below, otherwise the
selected day as now; `selectedDayIndex` is left untouched so returning to Days restores it).
When the Resupply tab is active, `#datasheet-title` is "Resupply plan" and `#datasheet-body`
renders one table from `computeResupplyLegs`:

| # | From → To | Distance | Ascent | Descent | Est. days | Food | Arrive |
|---|-----------|----------|--------|---------|-----------|------|--------|

- "Arrive" shows `Day N (date)` from `currentDays` when the camp plan has stops and a start date,
  else the column is omitted. `isLong` legs get the existing warning styling.
- A summary line above: `N stops · longest carry X km / Y days · Z kg food in total`.
- Web has no pace or daily-hours input; use 4 km/h and 8 h/day (constants named in one place,
  `RESUPPLY_DAILY_HOURS`). Adding inputs is out of scope.
- The existing `#resupply-section` collapsible under the Days tab is reduced to the same summary
  line plus "Edit in the Resupply tab", fed from the same legs, so the two never disagree.

  `initPlanViewer(trailId, preloadedTrail)`; their ids are `uw_…` and stable for the same file.
- Direction toggle: nothing to convert, ids are direction-agnostic.
