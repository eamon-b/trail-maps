Trail information is a guide, not a guarantee

Tracknotes shows trail routes, distances, elevation, water sources, huts, campsites, towns, map data from OpenStreetMap and others, plans and estimates (walking times, water carries, resupply), and comments and waypoints posted by other hikers. All of it can be incomplete, out of date or wrong. Tracks drift, water sources dry up, huts close, routes are rerouted or closed, and conditions change from day to day. Estimates are arithmetic on the figures you enter, not advice about what you can safely do.

You are responsible for your own safety. Check official sources (land managers, park and trail authorities, weather and fire services) before and during a walk, carry a paper map, navigation and contingency water, and do not rely on Tracknotes, on a phone or on a battery as your only means of finding your way or water.
