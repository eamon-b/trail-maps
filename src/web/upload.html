    Share with the community

    A shared route is listed for everyone on Trail Maps and in the Tracknotes app. It goes up straight away labelled Unverified, is reviewed automatically, and is marked Verified once a person has checked it. How sharing works

    It will be listed publicly as Unverified straight away and reviewed. The route, its text and your display name are public; recording times are not. The original GPX file, if you include it, is kept privately. You can edit or delete it later from its page.
